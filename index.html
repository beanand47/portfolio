hi, i'm Anand.

i'm a cs student who thinks about products the way some people think about films — frame by frame, asking why every decision was made the way it was.

my real obsession is the space where technology meets how people actually live and make decisions. product thinking, user behavior, brand experience, systems design — that's where i spend most of my mental energy.

lately i've been writing product teardowns, drafting prds for problems i find genuinely unsolved, running small experiments, and documenting what i'm learning in public. not because i have it figured out. but because i think the best way to develop product sense is to practice it visibly — where you're accountable to the thinking, not just the output.

i'm drawn to early-stage environments where the problems are still ambiguous, the constraints are real, and the decisions actually matter.

some of the things i've tried: teardown series, prd writeup, experiment log. some worked. most didn't. but i learned a lot.
