newsletters i actually read.

not a curated list to look smart. these are the ones that actually land in my brain. mostly product, tech, startups, and the occasional thing that has nothing to do with any of that.

lenny's newsletter weekly
by lenny rachitsky
deep dives on product, growth, and how the best pms actually think. the one i read start to finish every single week.
the pragmatic engineer biweekly
by gergely orosz
what's actually happening inside big tech — from an engineer who's been there. grounded, specific, no fluff.
product compass weekly
by bart jaworski
frameworks for product thinking without the hand-waving. practical and specific every time.
every daily
by various writers
a bundle of independent writers thinking seriously about tech, ai, and what comes next. quality varies but the ceiling is high.
dense discovery weekly
by kai brach
design, tech, culture. the one newsletter that consistently makes me slow down. always worth the time.
stratechery weekly
by ben thompson
strategy-level analysis of the tech industry. not casual reading — but always worth it once you're in.
the generalist weekly
by mario gabriele
deep research on the most interesting companies being built right now. long form done right.
no mercy / no malice weekly
by scott galloway
contrarian takes on business and culture. sometimes wrong, always interesting. never boring.
farnam street — brain food weekly
by shane parrish
mental models, decision making, and how to think more clearly. the one i come back to when i feel like i'm thinking sloppily.
tldr daily
by dan ni
the fastest way to stay current on tech and startup news. 5 minutes every morning. no noise.

if you write something worth reading — i'm always open to a recommendation.
